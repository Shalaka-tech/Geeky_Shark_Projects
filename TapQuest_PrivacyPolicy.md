# TapQuest Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 7, 2026**

TapQuest is developed by GeekyShark.

## Information We Collect

TapQuest does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or location.

TapQuest generates a user identifier and stores limited game-related information, such as game progress and scores, in order to provide app functionality, maintain player progress, and support features such as the leaderboard.

This information is stored using Supabase, our backend service provider.

## How We Use Information

The information collected by TapQuest is used only to provide and maintain the functionality of the app.

TapQuest does not use this information for advertising, marketing, or cross-app tracking.

## Data Sharing

TapQuest does not sell user data or share user data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data may be processed and stored by service providers used to operate the app, such as Supabase.

## Tracking and Advertising

TapQuest does not use advertising trackers, behavioral advertising, or cross-app tracking.

## Children's Privacy

TapQuest does not knowingly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location information from children.
